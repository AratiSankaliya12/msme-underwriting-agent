# Banking & Finance Glossary

Terms encountered during the MSME Underwriting Agent build.

---

**NBFC** : Non-Banking Financial Company. A financial institution that provides
loans and other banking services but does NOT hold a banking license.
Examples: Lendingkart, Indifi, Bajaj Finance.

**MSME** : Micro, Small and Medium Enterprise. Small businesses in India
(manufacturers, traders, service providers) that are a primary customer
segment for NBFCs.

**DSCR** : Debt Service Coverage Ratio. Measures whether a business earns
enough to repay its loans. Formula: Net Operating Income ÷ Total Debt Service.
A DSCR above 1.25 is generally considered healthy.

**ITR** : Income Tax Return. The annual tax filing that declares a business's
income to the Indian government. Used to verify declared revenue.

**GST Return** : Goods and Services Tax filing. Shows how much a business
sold (and therefore what its revenue should be). We compare this against
bank statement credits to detect discrepancies.

**Credit Memo** : The formal document a credit analyst writes summarizing
their assessment of a loan application — risk level, key findings, and
recommendation (approve/reject/escalate).

**Round-trip / Circular Transaction** : A fraud pattern where money is
sent OUT to Account X and comes BACK from Account X within a few days —
artificially inflating the account balance to look healthier than it is.

**Udyam Registration** : India's official MSME registration certificate.
Confirms the business is formally registered and what category it falls in.

**Co-lending** : A model where two lenders (e.g., an NBFC + a bank) jointly
fund a single loan, sharing the risk and the returns.
